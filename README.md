# C# Casino Game

## Description

This is a console-based casino game made in C#. The player starts with $500 and can play different casino games to try to increase their balance. The program keeps track of the player's money, wins, losses, ties, bets, and win streaks.

## Games

The casino has three different games:

* **Blackjack** - Players can hit, stand, or double down. The dealer draws until reaching at least 17.
* **Slots** - Three random symbols are selected. Matching symbols give the player a payout, with three Sevens being the jackpot.
* **Roulette** - Players can bet on red, black, odd, even, or a specific number from 0-36.

## Features

* Starts the player with $500
* Betting system that prevents invalid bets
* Blackjack with hit, stand, and double down
* Slot machine with different payouts
* Roulette with multiple betting options
* Player statistics
* Win and loss tracking
* Current and best win streak tracking
* Casino shop
* Lucky Charm that increases winnings by 10%
* VIP Card that gives an extra $25 after a win
* Random casino bonus events

## Casino Shop

The player can spend their money on two items:

**Lucky Charm - $200**

* Gives the player 10% more winnings.

**VIP Card - $300**

* Gives the player an extra $25 whenever they win.

Each item can only be purchased once.

## How to Play

1. Enter your name when the game starts.
2. You begin with $500.
3. Choose an option from the casino menu.
4. Select a game and enter your bet.
5. Play the game and receive your winnings or lose your bet.
6. Check your stats or visit the shop between games.
7. Continue playing until you choose to exit or run out of money.
